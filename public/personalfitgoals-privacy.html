ARISE Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026. Operator: Jagadish Reddy. Contact: jagadish.reddy1611@gmail.com.

Browser and cloud modes

Without signing in, training progress stays in this browser’s local storage. Signing in enables account-based cloud saving when the backend is configured. Browser progress is uploaded only when you choose Import browser plan into cloud. Downloads remain under your control.

Information stored

The account service stores your name, email, a password hash, sessions, session expiry, and potentially IP address and browser information. Passwords are not stored as readable text. Your account’s training data includes campaign dates, selected weeks, completed quests, weekly notes, baseline profile, race checklists, Winter Arc food preference and check-ins. Please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into free-text notes.

Optional WHOOP connection

WHOOP is optional and requires your authorization. Requested scopes cover recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts and offline token refresh. No profile or body scope is requested. WHOOP may return heart rate, HRV, sleep details, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, temperature, strain, activity and timestamps when available. The app stores the returned recent records and sync time. It fetches up to ten records per category when you press Sync now; it does not continuously track you.

WHOOP access and refresh tokens are encrypted on the server with AES-GCM. A short-lived secure cookie and single-use database state protect the authorization callback. Imported health data is stored in the configured Postgres database; it is not end-to-end encrypted.

Purpose and providers

Data supports account sign-in, saving your plan across devices, progress tracking and showing your authorized WHOOP readings. This implementation does not sell data, use it for advertising or train AI models. Scores do not automatically increase your training load.

The website and backend run on Vercel. Cloud records reside with the Postgres provider connected by the operator, intended to be Neon. Better Auth is a library running in the backend, not a separate hosted account service. WHOOP provides data you authorize. Hosting and database providers may process operational logs and backups under their policies.

Cookies, retention and security

The site uses session cookies for sign-in and a temporary WHOOP authorization cookie. Browser progress remains until you clear it. Cloud progress remains until deleted. Tokens and imported WHOOP snapshots remain until disconnected successfully or removed by the operator. Provider logs and backups can have separate retention periods. Transport uses HTTPS; access is restricted by authenticated account identity. No security measure is absolute.

Your controls

Export progress downloads training progress, not WHOOP records. Delete my cloud training progress removes that account’s training record; it leaves your account and WHOOP connection intact. Disconnect & delete imported data attempts WHOOP revocation, then removes tokens and imported snapshots on success or when authorization is already invalid. If revocation fails, the record is retained so you can retry. You may independently revoke the app in WHOOP.

Sign out ends the browser session and displays browser progress. For account deletion, access to imported WHOOP data, corrections or other privacy requests, contact the operator above. Requests will be verified before personal records are disclosed or removed. Deletion does not guarantee immediate removal from provider backups.

Audience and changes

This training tool is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The policy may change when features or service providers change; the effective date will be updated.
